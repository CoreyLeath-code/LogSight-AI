# Queue backlog response

1. Confirm producer rate, consumer lag, and retry volume for the affected topic or queue.
2. Identify the affected consumer group and correlate it with trace and deployment evidence.
3. Preserve offsets and the incident timeline before scaling consumers or replaying work.
4. Record the runbook version and repository revision used for the decision.
